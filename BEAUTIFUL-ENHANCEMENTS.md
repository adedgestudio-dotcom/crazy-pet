## 🌟 Key Visual Features

### Shadows
- **Soft**: 10px blur, 15% opacity - for subtle elevation
- **Medium**: 15px blur, 25% opacity - for standard cards
- **Strong**: 20px blur, 35% opacity - for hero CTAs

### Gradients
- **Linear gradients**: Orange-to-amber for warmth
- **Background gradients**: Cream tones for subtle depth
- **Overlay gradients**: White overlays for shine effects

### Borders
- **Accent borders**: Orange-100 → Orange-200/300 on hover
- **White borders**: 2-4px for image containers
- **Subtle borders**: Alpha transparency for glass effects

### Animations
- **Duration**: 300ms for most transitions
- **Easing**: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1) for smooth motion
- **Stagger**: 100-150ms delays for sequential animations
- **Pulse**: Decorative blobs with 1-2s delays
